How the rib stays exact

The plate is divided into cells that share their edges exactly, and each cell is shrunk by half a rib. Wherever two holes face each other, the web between them is one rib thick; where several meet it only gets thicker. Rounding a corner or dropping a small hole only ever removes hole, so neither can thin a rib. The border and the clearance round each screw hole are built the same way.

Styles

Classic lays a jittered grid over the plate and trims it around obstacles. Relaxed (6 sides) repeatedly moves each cell's centre to the middle of its cell, so cells even out and wrap around screw holes. Rosette starts with a ring of cells around each hole, the way spots ring a leopard's rosette, then relaxes.
